# Public projects

Here are some projects you can check out now: click the Github link at left to go to them directly. If you've heard us talking about something cool and it's not here, it's probably not accessible yet.

- **matlab-fluorescent-particle-image-analysis**: a script that can load, label, and analyze properties of particles in a series of images, with various options for cropping and region of interest specifications. 

- **matlab-chemists-tutorial**: a tutorial for plotting data, matrix math, curve fitting, image analysis and programming in Matlab, with examples from physical chemistry. An opportunity to practice your Matlab or your p-chem!

- **mtc** Our "Matlab toolbox for chemists": An extensible set of tools for doing many useful things in Matlab. Coming soon: modules for spectroscopy and isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC). 

- **Arduino "Basic Laboratory Instrument"**: A modular starting point for development of various lab instruments based on the Arduino Uno microcontroller, with hardware design for button inputs, LCD output, and a variety of peripheral inputs and outputs. Examples:
	- **arduino-bli**: The basic version, with software examples for servo control, reading and displaying calibrated analog data, and capable of serving as a low frequency TTL and sine wave function generator.
	- **arduino-bli-mono-controller**: The "BLI" deployed as a monochromator controller, driving a stepper motor and limit switch with adjustable sweep parameters and option for remote triggering.
	
- **Arduino spincoater**: Lives in our glovebox: makes use of a hard drive motor and solenoid-operated vacuum chuck.
